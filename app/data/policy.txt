CashGuard Finance Policy Reference

1. Credit Policy
- Auto-approve orders where projected credit utilisation is below 75%.
- Route to human review if projected credit utilisation is between 75% and 95%.
- Block the order if projected credit utilisation exceeds 95%, or if the
  customer's payment_risk is "high", regardless of utilisation.

2. Pricing Policy
- Any deviation between the submitted unit price and the contracted unit
  price must be flagged for human review, regardless of the size of the
  deviation (even $1 off contract is flagged).

3. Inventory Policy
- If the requested quantity for any line exceeds the available quantity in
  stock, flag the order for human review (fulfillment decision required).

4. Dispute Policy
- If a pricing dispute is valid and the financial impact is under $500,
  resolve with a credit memo.
- If a pricing dispute's financial impact is $500 or more, escalate to the
  sales team rather than resolving automatically.

5. Collections Policy
- Invoices that are 15+ days overdue require an urgent follow-up.
- Invoices that are 90+ days overdue should be considered for write-off
  review.
